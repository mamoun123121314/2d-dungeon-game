Living Dungeons

Eleven room themes, from flooded vaults to ember halls. Champions carry six elite traits — swift, shielded, leeching, volatile, giant, beckoning — and every fifth floor ends in a boss with a three-move arsenal.

Elemental Power

Fire burns, frost slows, venom rots and shock chains. Twenty-one talent ranks across nine branches let you bend the build your way, one point every third level.

The Descent

Kill fast to chain a combo up to ×8. Clear bounties, haggle with the Quartermaster, and past floor ten the dungeon starts stacking affixes against you.
